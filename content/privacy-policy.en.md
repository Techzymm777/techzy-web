## 4. Device data, repairs, trade-ins, and shop visits

This section covers the parts specific to buying and servicing electronics.

### 4.1 Walk-in and in-store customers

If you visit our shop, we may also collect:

- **Repair or warranty intake details:** your name, phone number, the device handed in, its serial number or IMEI, the reported fault, and the device passcode if you choose to provide one. This is recorded on a service job sheet.
- **CCTV footage** inside the shop and at the entrance, for security and loss prevention. Signage is displayed at the premises.
- **Guest WiFi data,** if you connect to our network: device MAC address, connection times, and data used.
- **Loyalty or membership data,** if you enrol: purchase history and points balance linked to your phone number.
- **In-store card and wallet payments** are handled through the bank or provider terminal. We keep only the last four digits, the card or wallet type, and the approval reference on our copy of the receipt.

You do not need to give an email address or ID document for an ordinary cash purchase. Where we do ask for ID, for example for an instalment plan or a formal tax invoice, we record only what the financing partner or tax rules require.

### 4.2 Repairs, warranty service, and trade-ins

- **Serial numbers and IMEI** are recorded against your purchase so we can confirm warranty eligibility and register the product with the manufacturer where required.
- **Devices left for service:** please back up your data and remove personal files before handing over any device. Where a fault cannot be diagnosed otherwise, our technicians may need to switch the device on and access it. We do not copy, keep, or share the contents of your device beyond what the repair requires, and we may ask you to sign a service authorisation form.
- **Screen locks and passcodes:** a passcode you provide for diagnosis is used only for that job and removed from our records once it closes.
- **Trade-ins and buy-backs:** we wipe or securely reset every trade-in device before resale or disposal. Please sign out of your accounts (for example iCloud, Google, or Microsoft) and remove any device lock before you trade in, as some locks cannot be cleared by us.

### 4.3 Linking your shop and online records

If you buy in the shop and also hold an online account, we may link the two records using your phone number or email address so that:

- your warranty and repair history is visible whichever channel you use
- loyalty points and instalment records stay in one place
- we can confirm proof of purchase without you producing a paper receipt

If you would rather your in-store purchases were not linked to your online account, tell our staff at the counter or email ayezaw@techzymm.com.

## 8. How we protect your data

- HTTPS/TLS encryption across the whole website
- Passwords stored hashed, never in plain text
- Role based access, so staff see only the data their job requires
- Card and wallet payments handled by licensed providers, not stored on our systems
- Regular backups, software updates, and access log review
- Physical files and job sheets kept in a locked area of the shop

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and notify the relevant authority where the law requires it.
